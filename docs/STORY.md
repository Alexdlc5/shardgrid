# Glimmerwood: Story Bible

*Agreed with the owner 2026-10-15. Supersedes the three-sentence story in DESIGN.md. Every line here is meant to be placed in the game; nothing needs a wall of text to work.*

Names checked by web search 2026-10-15 (no existing game, book or brand found): Glimmeroak, Lantern Moles, Granny Burr, Pale Bidder, Amber Memories, Underroot, Cinderthread Run, Undermoon, Scalefall Roost, Moonloom, Wickhold, Skywell, Wickway. Avoided: "Hollow Moon" (several novels and games), "Moth Moon" (an itch.io game). **Trademark check (2026-10-16):** USPTO clear for both, but an iOS game "Glimmerwood: Potion Sort" launched 2026-10-13; see [SETUP.md A13](SETUP.md#a13-trademark-check-5-minutes).

## How the story is told

- **Old Puddock's one-liners**: warm, funny, evasive. They read differently once you know the truth.
- **Mysterious text** with no narrator, in italics:
  - **Granny's chalk marks** on tunnel walls (board frame), signed "—B".
  - **Amber Memories**: rare crystals (3–4 per mine). Breaking one shows a mostly wordless picture of the past, with one caption. They fill the Codex and can be replayed at camp.
  - **The roots**, murmuring when the Rootline closes.
- **Only two dialogue scenes** in the whole game: Puddock's confession (Geode Gulch) and the reunion (Wickhold).
- Pictures first. Every scene is drawn in code like the rest of the art (proto/art.js).

## The backstory (what really happened)

1. The Glimmerwood is named for **the Glimmeroak**, a vast oak whose leaves sent sunlight down into its roots, where it hardened into crystal.
2. Deep below sits the **Rootheart**, a crystal the size of a pond that lets centuries of stored light out slowly. The **Lantern Moles**, led by **Granny Burr**, tend it; their fireflies drink off the excess.
3. Years ago a young, broke Puddock chipped a fist-sized **shard** off the heart and sold it to a cloaked stranger, **the Pale Bidder**, for pale coin. The coin built his market in the oak's hollow.
4. The chip broke the heart's seal. The shard **pulls** the heart's light toward itself, wherever it is. Light packed that tight turns to heat: a **rootfire** smouldered inside the roots all season. Warm soil in midwinter, leaves too bright. Puddock saw and said nothing.
5. The fire hollowed the taproot. On **the Night of the Fall**, an ordinary autumn gale brought the Glimmeroak down. Only the stump was left.
6. That night Granny Burr gave Puddock **her lantern and a letter** for her family, then led every Lantern Mole down. They froze the burning roots with **Frostspar**, then followed the light (and the fire) down into the **Underroot**. Puddock, ashamed, never delivered the letter.
7. The cracked heart now feeds the Underroot instead of the roots, so **the roots starve and reach for any light**, including Dibble's lantern. That is the Rootline. Breaking a vein gives light back and pushes them off.
8. The Pale Bidder is an old moth. Her people nested under **the Skywell**, a shaft open to the sky, until it caved in. Moths steer by the moon; generations hatched in the dark fly in spirals until they drop. She built them **the Undermoon**: their own scales spun around the stolen shard. Pilfermoths mistake lanterns for the moon, and their scales soak up glimmer and carry it home.
9. Granny Burr and the Lantern Moles reached the Undermoon to take the shard back, saw the little ones, and stayed to **tend it**, keeping its pull gentle enough that the Glimmeroak's roots survive.

The player learns all of this in pieces, in this order.

## Ring One: the Glimmeroak (mines that exist today)

Camp: at the stump.

**Prologue** (first launch, 4 panels with one caption each, skippable, shown once): the oak glowing at dusk · a red glow under the roots · lanterns going down into the ground · dawn, a stump, a toad with a cart.

**Meeting Puddock** (already in game): "Haven't seen one of those since the old oak fell." He looks at the lantern like he's seen a ghost.

**Home screen**: the stump in the forest backdrop; a **sapling** grows from it as total light released (lifetime gold) rises.

### Mossy Hollow: *The Stump*
- Puddock: "Old girl just got tired, that's all. Trees do." · "Lanterns and moles. Brings back… nothing. Brings back nothing." · "Folk sit on that stump. I don't."
- Amber: *The last leaf. It should not have been that bright.*
- Chalk: *↓ —B*

### Cinderroot Caverns: *Scorched* (roots burned hollow; embers are cooled sparks)

- Amber: *Smoke rose from the ground, not the sky.* · *The soil was warm in midwinter.*

### Gloamcap Grotto: *What Grows on the Dead* (mushrooms on burnt wood)

- Chalk, under ice: *Fire's frozen. Heart still leaks. Following it down. —B*

### Geode Gulch: *Sealed Things* (bubbles of escaped light, cooled to stone)
- Found in a geode, a receipt: *One shard, heartwood, fist-sized. Paid in full. Do not ask what for. —P.B.*

  - "I watched those leaves glow all autumn and said nothing."
  - "Your gran gave me this the night she went down. I've kept it on that shelf for years, too ashamed to deliver it."

- Puddock's haul lines from here on: "I sold a piece of her for a shop. You're buying her back, one crystal at a time." · "Fair as rain. Trying to be, anyway." · "Your gran would've liked your digging. Hated your haggling."

### Heartroot Deep: *The Heart*
- Amber: *There is a hollow where the shard was. It aches like a missing tooth.*
- Final chalk, past the heart: *The under-roots go everywhere. So did the fire. Keep your lantern lit. —B*
- At the stump, the first green shoot. Puddock: "Well. Would you look at that. Green." · "You're going after her. …Course you are. I'll pack the cart."

### The lead: the coin is a wing
Puddock holds his last unspent pale coin next to a Pilfermoth scale from the Codex. They match.
- "That's no coin. Never was. That's a *wing*."
- "Whoever paid me… the moths are theirs. And they've been carrying something down."

### Packing up camp (first move)
A short animated scene. **Every camp item is preserved exactly** and hops into Puddock's cart; the camp is unpacked in the same arrangement at the new site. Old mines stay playable.
- Puddock: "Careful with the shelf. That's twenty years of 'not for sale'." · "My cart's never gone further than the stump. Neither have I." · "Lantern lit? Good. You lead. I'll complain."

## The bridge: Cinderthread Run

Camp: inside the Underroot. The tunnel the rootfire burned on its way down; every scorch mark points the same way.
- Reveals: the fire was **pulled** (a piece of a heart calls to the rest); the moths are **carriers**, not thieves; a silhouette of the Bidder; the **Undermoon** glowing far below from a ledge.
- Puddock: "Every scorch mark points the same way. Fire doesn't do that. Not on its own." · "Mind the scales. They're warm. Scales shouldn't be warm." · "I sold a chip of the heart to a moth. Say it out loud and it sounds worse." · At the ledge: "Well. Somebody built themselves a moon."
- Amber: *The fire did not wander. It was called.* · *Wings folded like a cloak. A heart-shard held up like a candle.* · *A thousand small wings, each carrying a crumb of light.*
- Chalk: *Moths everywhere. Not attacking. Hauling. Following them. —B*
- Codex, pale scale: *Unknown. Pale. Warm to the touch. Faintly humming.*
- Roots: *Down here, someone else is hungry too.*

## Ring Two: the Undermoon

### Scalefall Roost: *The Spirals* (a moth nursery; scales drift like snow)
- Reveal: moths steer by the moon, and down here there is none.
- Puddock: "They're not attacking. They're… lost." · "Every one of them's flying in circles. Like me, after the oak."

### The Moonloom: *What It's Made Of* (the Undermoon woven from scales around the shard)
- Reveals: the Skywell cave-in; **Lantern Mole brackets bolted into the loom** (cliffhanger: the moles are helping).
- Puddock: "That's my shard. Right there. Wearing a moon like a coat." · "Those are mole brackets. Your gran's work. I'd know them anywhere."
- Amber: *Once there was a hole in the roof, and through it, the real one.* · *The roof fell. The sky went out. Only she remembers it.*
- Chalk: *Couldn't take it. Couldn't leave it. So we tend it. —B*

### Wickhold: *The Keepers* (the Lantern Moles' village around the Undermoon)
**Reunion scene:**
- Granny: "We came down to take it back. Then we saw the little ones."
- The Pale Bidder: "I did not want a heart. I wanted a sky. The heart was what was for sale."
- Puddock, to the Bidder: "You paid fair. I didn't sell fair. I knew it was hers."
- Granny: "Open the Skywell. Give them a real moon, and the shard can go home."

### Packing up camp (second move)
Same scene; **every camp item preserved and moved**, now to the floor of the collapsed Skywell. Granny Burr and the Lantern Moles join camp and hang their lanterns beside yours.
- Puddock: "Twice in one life. My cart's going to want a raise."

## The finale: the Skywell

The only cave at the final site. The shaft is choked with fallen rock and **you dig upward**. Special: rubble stone that cracks after neighbouring clears (DESIGN.md's "Puddock stone").

Finishing it:
1. The last rock falls; **real moonlight pours down**. The moths fly straight for the first time.
2. The Pale Bidder hands Dibble the shard.
3. The Lantern Moles relight **the Wickway**. Lantern Moles travelled by *lantern-stepping*: their fireflies know every tunnel the moles ever lit, and stepping into a lantern's light takes you to its twin. Moonlight runs down every old lantern line at once.
4. At camp a **ring of lanterns** lights up, one per cave dug. **Step into any lantern to play that mine.** This is the portal to every past cave.
5. Dibble lantern-steps to Heartroot Deep and sets the shard back. At the stump, the sapling shoots up into a **young Glimmeroak**.
- Puddock: "Well. Would you look at that. A whole sky."
- Roots: *Warm again.*
- Chalk, in Dibble's paw: *Found you, Gran. —D*

## After the finale: endless play and the tease

**Keep playing for high scores.** Every mine stays open through the Wickway. Each lantern shows that mine's **best haul**, and glows brighter the higher it is, so the ring itself is the trophy shelf. Daily Dig continues. New Puddock lines rotate here:
- "Beat your best in the Roost? Your gran says you can't."
- "That lantern's looking dim. Just saying."
- "Coins for crystals. Same as ever. Nicer view, though."

**The tease (shown once after the finale, then kept as visual hints):**
- The moths can see far now. They report **other great hearts glowing on the horizon, and some are dimming.**
- The Pale Bidder: *"I was not the only one buying."*
- Puddock, reading the moths' map: "Dimmest one's near my cousin's shop. Course it is." Puddock's cousin has been mentioned since the first haul screen ("Finer than my cousin's, anyway"). The cousin stays **unnamed** until the next Ring.
- Visual hints that stay at camp: Puddock's cart **half-packed**, a sign on it reading *Back soon (probably)*; one **dark lantern** in the Wickway ring marked with a little toad print. Tapping it: *"Not lit yet. Puddock keeps 'meaning to write'."*

## Pets: who the crystals kept

*"Crystals keep things, little mole. Sometimes they keep someone."* (Puddock)

When the Glimmeroak's light leaked into the ground, it cooled into crystal around whatever was nearby, and a few small round creatures were curled up asleep at the time. They're still asleep. Now and then a crystal in the tray has one faintly visible inside; clear the line through its cell and the crystal cracks, the pet pops out, and it's yours. If the dig ends first, the crystal goes back to the mine with it still asleep.

Odds (`petFor` in index.html): a pet can turn up once 3 crystals have been placed in a dig, one at a time. The **first** pet comes quickly: 2% a tray for the first 8 trays (about one dig), then 35% a tray, so it usually turns up in the second or third dig. After that pets are rare: about 1.8% of trays with six still to find, falling to about 0.7% with one left, so all seven take about 90 digs (a week or two of regular play; simulated median 89, 10–90% range 51–147). A mine's own pets are 6× likelier there.

| Pet | Turns up most in | Line |
|---|---|---|
| Moss Puff | Mossy Hollow | Sat on a crystal so long it grew moss. Then it was the moss. |
| Ember Mite | Cinderroot, Cinderthread, Heartroot | A spark that cooled into somebody. Still warm. |
| Glowcap Sprite | Gloamcap Grotto, Wickhold | Its antennae light up when it's happy. They're always lit. |
| Frost Bun | Frostpine Deep, the Moonloom | Fell asleep in a snowdrift and woke up in a crystal. Still sleepy. |
| Pebble Beetle | Geode Gulch, the Skywell | Carries its house on its back, and a few spare pebbles. |
| Tiny Moth | Scalefall Roost, the Moonloom, the Skywell | Too small to fly in circles. Flies in little loops instead. |
| Geode Hedgehog | Geode Gulch, Heartroot | Amethyst spines. Doesn't like hugs. Allows them. |

The pet you pick in the wardrobe sits beside Dibble at camp and watches from the corner of the board while you dig. Puddock sells tiny clothes for them in a Pets tab ("Tiny clothes for tiny friends. Everything fits. They're all round."). The pets are never explained further: they're a small mercy the fire left behind, not a plot thread. Names, lines and home mines live in `proto/story.js` (`PETS`), the art in `proto/art.js` (`pet`), the odds in `petFor` in `proto/index.html`.

## Expanding later (Ring Three and on)

Each Ring follows the same shape: **move camp (all items preserved) → three mines → one final cave → light a new lantern in the Wickway.** Dark lanterns in the ring are the visible roadmap. Open threads: the other buyers, the dimming hearts, Puddock's cousin.

## Where the story lives in the game (build checklist)

**Built 2026-10-15: the whole story, Ring One through the finale.** Text and story rules in `proto/story.js` (`SCENES`, `nextScene`, `MAP_GATE`); pictures in `proto/art.js` (`memoryArt` kinds, `stumpArt`, `granny`, `bidder`, `darkLantern`, three camp sites); mines in `sim/generator.json`.
How it plays: every mine has 3 Amber Memories (33 in all), dealt rarely and in story order. After a sale, `nextScene` plays at most one scene: confession (receipt found) → sprout → lead + first camp move (Heartroot Deep done and confessed; Cinderthread Run is given) → ledge (Cinderthread done; Scalefall Roost map goes on sale) → Moonloom and Wickhold maps unlock as the previous mine's memories are found → reunion + second camp move (Wickhold done; the Skywell is given; Granny joins camp) → finale (Skywell done). After the finale, the mine list becomes the Wickway: every mine stays playable, each card's lantern glows with that mine's best haul, the last card is the dark toad-print lantern, and Puddock's cart is half-packed at camp.
Notes:
- The stump sits in a panel on the home card (the forest backdrop is hidden behind the card on phones); tap it, or the camp button, to replay memories.
- Ring Two mines reuse the existing 12 gem types (the camp shelf holds 12) with new shapes, mixes and richer veins; each has its own chalk, Puddock lines and music key.
- The Skywell's special is rubble stone (Skystone, DESIGN.md's "Puddock stone"): whole pieces of grey rock that don't break with their line. A clear through or beside a stone cracks it; the second crack breaks it (`stoneBreak` in game.js, `stone_break` in sim.py). Only the Skywell deals it. The board is lit from the top and Granny's chalk sits up there too, so you dig toward the light. Skystone isn't a gem, so it stays off the camp shelf.
- Ring Two map prices (80k / 120k / 170k gold) and timers were set with `python sim/sim.py --timed`; they have not been playtested by people.
- Lines written beyond this document are all in `story.js` for review: the extra memory captions, the Geode Gulch, Heartroot, Wickhold and Skywell Puddock lines, the Gulch, Wickhold and Skywell chalk, Granny's camp lines, and the post-game lines.
